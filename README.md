# 謀道引階（DRS 428685382） / Mou dao yin jie: Transcription Dataset

底本為哈佛燕京圖書館藏本 DRS 428685382，索書號 RAREB TA 1980.2 2470.3。題葉著錄為光緒十五年己丑（1889），並印「濟南府同文齋藏板」。 [館目](https://id.lib.harvard.edu/alma/990094317070203941/catalog) · [固定掃描](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:Harvard_drs_428685382_%E8%AC%80%E9%81%93%E5%BC%95%E9%9A%8E.pdf&oldid=1293876337) · [館藏 IIIF](https://iiif.lib.harvard.edu/manifests/view/drs:428685382)

《謀道引階》是秀華陽（Francis Huberty James）所著的一卷晚清漢文基督教小冊。這一木刻本題葉署光緒十五年（1889），刊記為濟南府同文齋藏板。正文以問答開篇，先問普世之人由誰生養，再引用《詩》《書》，解說儒書所稱的「天」與上帝的關係。中段討論天人感通、良心辨別善惡，以及人如何悔過、行善與祈禱。卷末的〈謀道者早晚禱文〉以第一人稱感謝上帝的生養與教誨，祈求改過並為來生預備。這組問答與禱文可供研究晚清傳教者如何以儒家經典與道德語彙講解基督教信仰和日常實踐。

本資料集以哈佛燕京藏本 DRS 428685382 為底本，共取 26 幅固定影像，其中 15 幅用於轉錄正文，提供 JSON、TXT、TEI 和逐項覆核紀錄。此版為 AI 校讀稿，263 項比對中保留 31 項未解。人工全文二校待完成。

Mou dao yin jie is a one-volume late Qing Chinese Christian tract by Xiu Huayang (Francis Huberty James). The title leaf of this woodblock edition is dated 1889 (Guangxu 15) and bears the imprint of Tongwenzhai in Jinan Prefecture, Shandong. The dialogue opens by asking who gives people life and sustains them. It cites the Book of Odes and the Book of Documents and explains the relationship between Heaven in the Confucian classics and God (Shangdi). The questions then turn to how Heaven and human beings respond to one another, how conscience distinguishes good from evil, and how people can repent, do good, and pray. The closing Morning and Evening Prayer for Seekers of the Way thanks God in the first person for giving and sustaining life and for teaching, then asks for help in amending one’s conduct and preparing for the life to come. The dialogue and prayer offer material for studying how late Qing missionaries used the Confucian classics and their moral vocabulary to explain Christian belief and daily practice.

This dataset uses 26 fixed images from Harvard-Yenching Library DRS 428685382. The main text is transcribed from 15 pages. The AI-reviewed JSON, TXT and TEI transcription retains 31 unresolved comparison items. Full human collation is outstanding.

## 資料與校讀

- data/document.json 保留歷史文字、原圖位置、疑讀與缺口。
- data/text.txt 與 data/text.tei.xml 為全文與 TEI，附文字單位的對應檔。
- data/review.json 保留 263 項比對、31 項未解及採用紀錄。
- data/manifest.json 與 PACKAGE-SHA256SUMS.txt 記錄固定發行的檔案雜湊。

固定底本 26 幅。p003 為題葉及刊記，p004 為大字題辭，p005–p007 為序，p009–p023 為正文，含 p020 起的〈謀道者早晚禱文〉。p005–p024 保存可辨版心，p008、p024 框內沒有獨立文字。封面題簽、後加記號、襯葉及透印依圖面所見記錄。

The fixed source consists of 26 images. The title leaf and imprint are on p003, a prefatory statement in large characters on p004, the preface on p005–p007, and the text on p009–p023, including the prayer beginning on p020. Visible running titles and folio numbers in p005–p024 are recorded separately. On p008 and p024, printing is confined to the fold margin. The cover label, later marks, endpapers, and show-through are described as visible.

初錄與第二路讀稿各以同一批固定原圖為據。Claude 完成第 1–20 幅初錄與第 1–18 幅第二路讀稿。Codex 完成第 21–26 幅初錄與第 19–26 幅第二路讀稿。Codex 依原圖覆核 263 項比對。程式列出的 263 項比對均有逐項紀錄。採入 10 項有影像依據的更正，保留未解位置與候選。另有 5 項異讀保留原讀及候選。程式驗收確認公開 JSON、TXT 和 TEI 的逐欄內容一致。TEI 通過 P5 4.12.0 Relax NG。人工全文二校、專家審定和準確率量測待完成。

The two independent readings used the same fixed source images. Claude produced the first reading for images 1–20 and the second reading for images 1–18. Codex produced the first reading for images 21–26 and the second reading for images 19–26. Codex reviewed 263 comparison items against the images. The dataset records 263 comparison items. The edition incorporates 10 corrections supported by the images. The initial readings and alternatives remain recorded for 5 further proposed changes. Automated validation confirmed that the transcription content agrees column by column across JSON, TXT and TEI. The TEI passed P5 4.12.0 Relax NG validation. Full human collation, expert assessment and accuracy measurement are outstanding.
